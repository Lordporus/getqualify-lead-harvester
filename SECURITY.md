## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of this repository and lead harvesting pipeline seriously. If you identify a potential security vulnerability, credential leak, or sensitive exposure, please **do NOT report it in public GitHub issues, discussions, or pull requests**.

Instead, report vulnerabilities privately through either of the following channels:

1. **GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting (Preferred):**
   - Navigate to the repository's [Security Tab](https://github.com/Lordporus/getqualify-lead-harvester/security).
   - Click on **Advisories** -> **Report a vulnerability**.
   - Provide reproduction steps, potential impact, and suggested remediations.

2. **Direct Maintainer Email:**
   - Email: **[purushottam21028@gmail.com](mailto:purushottam21028@gmail.com)**
   - Subject: `[SECURITY] Potential vulnerability in getqualify-lead-harvester`
   - Include relevant technical details and proof-of-concept code.

### Response Timelines & SLA
- **Initial Acknowledgement:** Within 48 hours of submission.
- **Triage & Assessment:** Within 5 business days.
- **Remediation & Advisory Release:** Coordinated disclosure after fix validation.
